Tokyo Boom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

English

Tokyo Boom does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. We run no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

  • On your device: your scores, cleared stages and settings are kept only on your iPhone.
  • Game Center: if you sign in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to show the rankings. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are handled by the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders: reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, only if you allow them. Nothing is sent to us.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: see the support page.
